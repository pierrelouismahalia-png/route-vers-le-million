Why I left my bank
for Wealthsimple

Quebec, Canada · 5 min read · Platforms · Fees · Brokerage

"At first, I thought traditional banks were automatically the best choice. Until I realized that fees were eating up a significant portion of my small investments."

At first, I was investing with RBC and other traditional financial institutions. Honestly, I didn't really know the differences between brokerage platforms at the time. In my mind, "real banks" were automatically the best choice.

Over time, I realized there were often transaction fees. And when you start investing with small amounts, those fees can become very discouraging.

The problem with fees on small amounts

Someone investing $1,000 at once will feel less impact from a $9.99 commission. But when you start with $50 or $60 at a time, that same fee takes up an enormous share.

The real impact of fees on $50

Amount invested 50,00 $
Traditional bank commission - 9,99 $
Amount actually invested 40,01 $
Immediate loss to fees 20% of your money

"The most important thing is to start investing, even with small amounts. Even $50 can double and more over several years."

That's mainly why I chose Wealthsimple. The fact that there's generally no commission on transactions helped me stay consistent in my investing.
