Home  /  My Story

I don't have all
the answers.

"This blog doesn't exist to give lessons. It exists to connect with people who, like me, have an unconventional path and a deep desire to do better financially."

I'm not a financial planner or an investment expert. I'm simply a curious woman, passionate about personal finance, who decided to share her journey, her thoughts, and her trial and errors.

I didn't come from a wealthy family. I was a single mom at a very young age. I had no handout, no magic shortcut, no big head start in life. For a long time, I truly believed money was for other people. For me, having $20,000 in savings would already have been something huge.

And yet.

How I Started

At a certain point in my life, I was working as a consultant while taking on a few small contracts on the side under my own name. Honestly, I had no grand plan. I simply wanted to make ends meet.

I built my own website with very little resources. I learned SEO, online marketing, social media. And gradually, clients started coming.

Then one day, I realized something that completely changed my perspective: the income from my small contracts was exceeding my main salary.

"I was living below my means. And that's how, gradually, I managed to save $100,000 since the pandemic."

What That Amount Changed

That amount completely transformed the way I see money. Not just from a financial standpoint, but mentally too. I understood that money wasn't something to fear or reserved for an elite.

So I started listening to podcasts, reading finance books, and joining an investment book club. TFSA, RRSP, ETFs, compound interest — words no one had ever explained to me in school.

What School Never Taught Me

Today, what fascinates me isn't just saving. It's understanding how to build real financial freedom. I deeply believe it's possible for ordinary people to become more financially solid, even without an extraordinary income.

I also believe strongly in the power of small amounts invested with discipline. Whether through self-employment, extra income, or a few hundred dollars a month in passive income — everything becomes powerful when you invest consistently over many years.

Don't Be Afraid to Start Over

I know what it's like to go to school with a child to care for, to work, to be exhausted. I know what it feels like to think you're behind everyone else.

But sometimes, accepting temporary hardship to build something more solid completely changes a life.

This blog is my financial lab. I'll be sharing my ideas, my reflections, my successes, my mistakes, and my financial adventures.

And if some of you see yourselves in this… Welcome to my madness.
